Basement water damage cleanup in Lorain is an emergency when water is rising, and the call line is answered 24/7. Stay off the stairs if water is touching the panel or outlets, shut off the main breaker only if it is dry and reachable, and call. Move valuables upstairs and take photos of the water level before anything else.

What the cleanup involves

  1. Pump or extract standing water, starting at the deepest point.
  2. Remove wet carpet, pad, insulation and baseboards when needed.
  3. Clean and treat the slab and framing.
  4. Dry the space with dehumidifiers and air movers, with extra capacity because below-grade rooms hold moisture.
  5. Verify readings before rebuilding.

Finished basements and what is in them

Finished space adds drywall, carpet, trim and often a bathroom. Water wicks up drywall far above the visible line, and a clean cut a foot or two above the water mark is common practice. Furniture, boxes and stored items sit on the floor in most basements and take the first hit.

A sump pump that stops working is the easiest failure to prevent. Test it before heavy rain, check the discharge line, and consider a battery backup. Wiring and appliances near the water should be left alone until the power is off.

What to do in the first hour

  • Photograph the water level on walls and stored items.
  • Turn off power to the basement at the panel only if the panel is dry and not in the water.
  • Move belongings from low shelves and the floor to upstairs rooms.
  • Do not run a household vacuum on the water.
  • Keep children and pets out of the space.
  • Call dispatch and describe the depth and the likely source.

If water is from the ground or the drain, assume it is contaminated and wear boots and gloves. Do not use the furnace or water heater if they stand in water. Wait for a crew or a licensed professional to say it is safe.

What basement cleanup costs depend on

  • Depth and area. Larger areas take more pump and extraction time.
  • Water type. Contamination adds removal.
  • Finished versus unfinished. Finished space has more to remove and rebuild.
  • Contents. Stored items often cannot be saved.
  • Pump or drain repair. A plumber prices that.
