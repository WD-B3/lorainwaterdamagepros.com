A slab leak is a supply line failing beneath a concrete floor. You may never see a puddle, but the signs are there in the water meter, in the floor and in the noise.

Slab leak water damage in Lorain is a hidden-water problem, and calls are answered 24/7 once it becomes obvious. If the meter dial keeps moving with every tap closed, a hot spot appears on the floor or flooring starts to lift, close the main valve and call. Slab leaks can run for weeks, so shutting the water off protects the house while a plumber finds the line.

How to spot a slab leak

  • The water meter turns when every fixture and appliance is off.
  • A warm area on the floor, which suggests a hot water line is leaking.
  • The sound of running water when nothing is on.
  • Wet carpet, buckled flooring or damp baseboards with no visible source.
  • A sudden jump in your water bill.

Water bills tell part of the story, but a higher bill alone does not prove a slab leak. A meter test with every fixture off is the quicker check. Additions, garages and some single-story homes are built on slabs, so ask a plumber what your house sits on if you are unsure.

What a slab leak does to a house

Water finds the path of least resistance, which may be along the slab edge, up through a crack or under the flooring. The result is wet subfloor, warped planks, damp walls at the base and sometimes mold. Because the source is below the concrete, the repair involves a plumber locating the line with acoustic or thermal tools.

What cleanup follows

Once the water is shut off, crews extract what is standing, lift wet flooring and dry the slab surface, baseboards and lower wall cavities. The concrete itself holds moisture, so drying can take a while, and moisture readings decide when new flooring can go in.

What to do while you wait for a plumber

  • Close the main valve so the leak stops.
  • Move rugs and furniture off the damp area.
  • Open windows and run fans to move air across the floor.
  • Do not drill or cut the slab yourself.
  • Take dated photos of the damp spots and of the meter dial.

A slab leak is a plumbing repair first and a restoration job second. Locating the line comes before drying, and the plumber needs the water off at least long enough to test. Plan the dry-out so it starts as soon as the repair is complete.

What slab leak cleanup costs depend on

  • Duration of the leak. Long leaks soak the slab edge and walls.
  • Flooring. Hardwood and laminate suffer more than tile.
  • Square footage affected. Water travels under flooring farther than it shows.
  • Drying time. Concrete releases moisture slowly.
  • Plumbing repair. The plumber prices locating and fixing the line.
