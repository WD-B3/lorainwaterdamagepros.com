Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the site and the phone line collect, and what we do with it. It was last updated on November 9, 2026.

Call (440) 276-9091See all services

What this site collects

This site has no forms, no accounts and no comment fields, so it does not ask you to type personal details. Like any website, our hosting provider may record technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, which is used to deliver and secure the site.

Phone calls

When you call (440) 276-9091, we may log details of the call such as the time, the length and the number that called. What you tell dispatch, such as your address and the type of water damage, is used to arrange service and to follow up on your request. We do not sell it.

Third-party services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and several pages include a Google Maps embed. Those services receive your IP address and browser details when the content loads, under their own privacy policies. This site does not run advertising pixels.

How information is used and shared

We use information to answer your call, arrange work, communicate about a job and keep records. We share it with subcontractors, suppliers or insurers only when needed for your job or when you ask. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can browse this site without giving us any personal information. You may ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing contact@lorainwaterdamagepros.com. Some records may need to be kept for legal or insurance reasons.

Children

This site is meant for adults who own or manage property. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions can go to contact@lorainwaterdamagepros.com or to 3221 Oberlin Avenue, Lorain, OH 44053.
