Carpet drinks water fast and holds it longer than you would think. The deciding factors are the kind of water, how long it sat and what is under the carpet.

Carpet water damage restoration in Lorain depends on the type of water. Clean water from a supply line within a day or so often leaves carpet that can be extracted and dried in place. Water from a sewer, drain or outside flood leaves carpet and pad that are normally removed. Pull furniture off wet carpet and put foil or plastic under the legs to stop stains.

Carpet and pad are different problems

Carpet is the surface, and pad is the foam or fiber layer underneath. The pad acts like a sponge and takes the longest to dry. In many cases the pad is discarded and the carpet is lifted, dried and put back. Pad is relatively inexpensive to replace, so crews do not take risks with it.

How wet carpet is dried

  1. Extract water using a weighted extractor or a truck-mounted unit.
  2. Lift an edge and check the pad and the subfloor with a moisture meter.
  3. Float the carpet, meaning it is raised and supported on air movers, or remove the pad.
  4. Dry the room with dehumidifiers and fans.
  5. Re-stretch and secure the carpet when readings are dry.

Why speed matters for carpet

Over time, wet carpet backing can delaminate, dyes can bleed and tack strips can rust. Odors build as wet pad stays damp. Moisture below the carpet can also wick into walls, which is why a floor reading is part of the job.

Carpet in a basement behaves differently from a bedroom because the slab below holds moisture. The basement page explains what to look for in below-grade rooms, and the drying page explains the equipment.

What to do before the crew arrives

  • Stop the water at its source.
  • Take furniture off the carpet, or put foil or plastic under the legs.
  • Blot standing water with towels. Do not use a household vacuum.
  • Run fans and open interior doors to move air.
  • Take dated photos of the room and the carpet.

Avoid walking on wet carpet, since foot traffic grinds water and dirt into the backing and spreads stains. Remove wet rugs from rooms and hang them to dry flat. If the water came from a drain or the outdoors, keep children and pets out until a crew has looked at it.

What carpet cleanup costs depend on

  • Square footage. Whole rooms cost more than a spot.
  • Water category. Contaminated water means disposal instead of drying.
  • Pad type. Thicker pad costs more to replace.
  • Subfloor. Wet plywood or concrete adds drying.
  • Carpet age and quality. Older carpet may not be worth lifting.
