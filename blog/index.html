Water Damage Guides for Lorain Homeowners

Practical answers to the questions that come up after a leak or a flood, written for Lorain homeowners, renters and landlords.

Call (440) 276-9091See all services

Most people look up water damage after something has already gone wrong. The guides below are written for that moment. They cover what insurance usually covers, how to document a loss, what mold needs to grow, how to read an estimate and what Ohio rules say about selling a house or renting one.

Each guide ends with the next step to take and, where it matters, the local office to ask. When a question depends on your policy, your lease or your city, the guide says so and tells you where to look. Nothing here replaces advice from your insurer, a lawyer or a physician.

If water is in the house right now, skip the reading and call. Dispatch is answered every hour of every day, and the first minutes matter more than any guide.
