What to do when water is spreading in Vermilion

Lakefront and canal-front homes can go from damp to flooded quickly, so act in this order while the dispatcher is answering.

Find the source

Close the main shutoff, or the valve under the fixture. If water is coming in from outside, stay clear of the low rooms.

Power safety

Turn off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry and reachable. Otherwise call the utility or 911 first.

Lift valuables

Raise papers, electronics and boxes off the floor and move rugs.

Respect the water

Keep children and pets out of lake, river or drain water.
