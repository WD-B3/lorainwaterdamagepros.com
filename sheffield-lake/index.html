Winter first: what the lake does to pipes

Cold air coming off Lake Erie settles on exterior walls and unheated garages, and pipes in those spaces freeze first. The thaw is when the damage shows, because a split pipe leaks once the ice melts. The frozen pipe page covers the steps in detail.

Sheffield Lake adds one local detail. The city's water department thaws frozen meters, but the city says frozen pipes inside a home are the owner's responsibility. That means a split pipe is a plumber and a drying job, and the water department has no after-hours staff to call at night.

When water is spreading in Sheffield Lake

Water spreads fast in a lakeshore home, and each minute matters. Take the steps below while the line connects you to a dispatcher.

Stop the source

Close the main shutoff, usually where the line enters the house. For a toilet or sink, close the valve beneath it.

Cut power only if it is safe

Turn off power to wet rooms only from a dry panel that you can reach without wading. If you cannot, call the utility or 911.

Lift what you can

Raise valuables, papers and electronics off the floor and move rugs to dry ground.

Stay out of dirty water

Keep children and pets out of water from a drain or the lake.
